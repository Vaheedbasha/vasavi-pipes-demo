# 2. Verified Product Portfolio & Technical Standards

The company manufactures five primary product lines conforming to Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) and American Society for Testing and Materials (ASTM):

### 2.1 uPVC Water & Pressure Pipes
* **Indian Standard:** IS 4985:2000
* **Size Range:** 20 mm to 200 mm outer diameter
* **Pressure Ratings:** 2.5 Kgf/cm², 4 Kgf/cm², and 6 Kgf/cm² (Class 1, 2, and 3)
* **Applications:** Potable water supply networks, agricultural irrigation, and municipal mains.
* **Key Properties:** Mirror-smooth interior bore, zero scaling, high hydrostatic strength, alkali immune.

### 2.2 Plumbing Pipes
* **Indian Standard:** IS 4985:2000
* **Size Range:** 20 mm to 50 mm
* **Applications:** Residential towers, commercial complexes, and domestic plumbing.
* **Key Properties:** Precision solvent socket fitment, high burst pressure resistance, leak-free lifespan.

### 2.3 Rigid PVC Electrical Conduits
* **Indian Standard:** IS 9537 (Part-3): 1983
* **Size Range:** 16 mm to 63 mm
* **Applications:** Surface and concealed electrical wiring in buildings and industrial plants.
* **Key Properties:** High dielectric insulation, self-extinguishing flame retardant (FR), high crush tolerance.

### 2.4 Blue Threaded Column Pipes
* **Standard:** ASTM-D-1785
* **Pressure Schedule:** Sch-40 & Sch-80
* **Size Range:** ½ inch to 2 inches
* **Applications:** Borewell submersible pump installations and high-depth groundwater lift.
* **Key Properties:** CNC square male-female threads, high torsional load capacity, zero submerged corrosion.

### 2.5 Ivory Threaded Casing Pipes
* **Standard:** ASTM-D-1785
* **Pressure Schedule:** Sch-40 & Sch-80
* **Size Range:** ½ inch to 2 inches
* **Applications:** Deep well casing and aquifer borehole stabilization.
* **Key Properties:** High collapse resistance in rocky and alluvial strata, impact-absorbing wall structure.
