ADVANCED MANUFACTURING

Precision Extrusion at Our Guntakal Plant

Continuous twin-screw extrusion technology ensuring consistent wall thickness, seamless surfaces, and high burst strength.

STEP 01
MATERIAL PURITY

Virgin Polymer Compounding

100% virgin PVC suspension resin compounded with certified heat stabilizers and impact modifiers for high burst and tensile performance.

STEP 02
CONTROLLED EXTRUSION

Twin-Screw Extrusion

Computerized multi-zone thermal control delivers continuous melt homogenization, consistent wall thickness, and zero internal voids.

STEP 03
CALIBRATED ROUNDNESS

Vacuum Calibration & Cooling

Precision vacuum sizing tanks and refrigerated water spray baths lock in exact outer diameters, circularity, and smooth inner bore surfaces.

STEP 04
THREAD INTEGRITY

CNC Threading & Inspection

Dedicated CNC lathes cut square threads on borewell column pipes, followed by gauge verification, drop-weight tests, and hydrostatic pressure checks.

Strict Batch Quality Control: Every extrusion run is monitored for dimensional consistency and internal hydrostatic burst pressure before packing.

QUALITY ASSURANCE

Tested to Withstand High Pressure & Harsh Soil

Piping failure underground or inside concrete structures causes extensive damage. That is why Vasavi Pipes applies continuous dimensional and pressure verification at every extrusion stage.

From virgin polymer compounding through automated cooling to CNC socket threading, our inspection procedures ensure that water networks, electrical cabling, and submersible pump strings perform reliably in the field.

Hydrostatic Pressure Testing Batches are hydrostatically pressure tested to verify burst strength and joint water-tightness before dispatch.
Drop-Weight Impact Resistance Formulations withstand transit vibration and trench backfilling impact without hairline fractures.
CNC Thread Gauge Calibration Square threads and sealing ring seats on column pipes are gauge-calibrated for secure pump suspension.
Smooth Bore Hydraulic Efficiency Low friction coefficient reduces pumping head loss and resists mineral encrustation over time.
