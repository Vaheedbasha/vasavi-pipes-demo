ABOUT VASAVI PLAST · ESTABLISHED 2006

18+ Years of Industrial Extrusion Excellence

Established in 2006 by the Sathrasala family, Vasavi Plast Industries (Vasavi Pipes) is an established industrial manufacturer in Guntakal, Andhra Pradesh. We produce high-strength uPVC water pipes, rigid electrical conduits, and heavy-duty borewell column pipes.

With over 18 years of proven field reliability, every pipe is extruded using 100% virgin polymer to deliver high burst resistance, zero scaling, and decades of leak-proof performance for agriculture, commercial construction, and plumbing infrastructure.

100% Virgin Polymer

QUALITY ASSURANCE

Tested to Withstand High Pressure & Harsh Soil

Piping failure underground or inside concrete walls causes expensive damage. That is why Vasavi Plast applies rigorous quality testing at every extrusion stage.

From raw material compounding to final CNC socket threading, our quality checks ensure your water lines, electrical wires, and submersible pumps stay protected for decades.

Hydrostatic Pressure Burst Test Tested beyond rated working pressures (2.5, 4 & 6 Kgf/cm²) to guarantee leak-free water transfer.
Drop-Weight Impact Resistance Pipes resist heavy accidental impacts during handling, transport, and trench backfilling without cracking.
CNC Thread Gauge Calibration Square threads and rubber O-ring grooves are gauge-verified to hold heavy borewell pump strings safely.
Mirror-Smooth Bore (Low Hazen-Williams 'C' Factor) Friction-free inner wall prevents mineral scaling and reduces electric pumping costs by up to 20%.
